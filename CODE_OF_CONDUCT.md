# Code of Conduct

**Last reviewed:** 2026-08-02  
**Status:** Project-specific professional conduct policy (not a copy of an external licensed CoC).

## Our standard

NorthCare AI is a collaborative health-technology project. Contributors are expected to act with respect, professionalism, and care for the dignity of patients, caregivers, and frontline workers — especially in the Northern Ghana context.

## Expected behaviour

- Communicate respectfully and constructively.  
- Assume good intent; disagree without personal attacks.  
- Practise cultural sensitivity; avoid stereotypes and shaming language.  
- Treat patient dignity as non-negotiable in copy, imagery, and demos.  
- Handle health-related information responsibly (synthetic data only in development).  
- Credit others’ work and respect licences (including map attribution).  

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, discrimination, or intimidation of any kind.  
- Sharing real patient data or private credentials.  
- Inventing or misrepresenting medical guidance as approved fact.  
- Deliberately introducing malware, sabotage, or reckless security risk.  
- Sustained disruptive behaviour in project channels.

## Reporting

Raise concerns with the project maintainers through the privately designated channel. Do not post sensitive details in public issues.
