# NorthCare Reach — Short Pitch (30–45 seconds)

**Last updated:** 2026-08-03  

Many families in Northern Ghana still use basic phones. NorthCare AI already helps frontline workers deliver offline-first care, but people without smartphones cannot start that journey themselves. NorthCare Reach closes that gap with a **simulated USSD** path: a community member submits a synthetic request, profession-based routing places it with an eligible worker, and the worker acknowledges and responds in the Community Requests Centre. Human review stays central — the system does not diagnose, dispatch an ambulance, or invent clinical severity. Live telecom, SMS, and emergency-service integrations remain future partner work. Today, Reach proves the complete access story safely and privately.
