# Synthetic Data Policy

**Purpose:** Keep development and demos free of real patient information.  
**Status:** Active  
**Last reviewed:** 2026-08-02  

## Rules

- All development clients must be fictional.  
- Avoid identifiable real people (including recognisable photos of real patients).  
- Do not copy medical records.  
- Use clearly synthetic phone numbers and identifiers.  
- Do not use real facility credentials.  
- Do not store sensitive demonstration information in logs.  
- Client avatars should use initials or category illustrations by default.  
- Demo data must be resettable.  
- Audio fixtures must not contain real patient cases.  
- Generated case examples must be labelled **synthetic**.
