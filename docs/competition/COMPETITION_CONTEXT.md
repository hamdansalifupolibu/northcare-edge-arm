# Competition Context

## Competition

**Name:** UNICEF StartUp Lab — AI for Nurturing Care Hackathon

**Organisers:** UNICEF, MEST Africa, HOPin Academy

**Application Deadline:** 11 August 2026

## Challenge Focus

Accelerating maternal, newborn and under-five survival in Northern Ghana through the practical and responsible use of artificial intelligence at the community level.

## Official Challenge Areas

- Early-risk detection
- Localised nutrition intelligence
- Last-mile referral tracking
- Smarter CHPS workflows
- Practical support for frontline health workers
- Practical support for caregivers
- Low-connectivity operation
- Responsible application of AI

## Intended Outcomes

- A share of GHS 110,000 in cash prizes
- A sponsored three-day bootcamp in Tamale
- Mentorship from UNICEF, MEST Africa and HOPin Academy
- Possible pathways to incubation, acceleration and field piloting

## Submission Expectations

- A mandatory GitHub repository containing the source code
- Well-organised and clearly documented codebase
- Easy to inspect, reproducible and free of committed secrets
- Technically credible and suitable for demonstration
- Suitable for later development
- Understandable to judges and developers

## Why NorthCare AI Fits the Challenge

NorthCare AI directly addresses every official challenge area:

| Challenge Area | NorthCare AI Response |
|---|---|
| Early-risk detection | Deterministic danger-sign screening with explainable Red/Amber/Green prioritisation |
| Localised nutrition intelligence | Local food diversity assessment, seasonal meal guidance, Dagbanli audio counselling |
| Last-mile referral tracking | End-to-end referral lifecycle with offline QR Referral Passport and status tracking |
| Smarter CHPS workflows | Voice-to-Care capture, guided screening, client management, follow-up scheduling |
| Frontline worker support | Offline-first tools that reduce form burden and surface approved guidance |
| Caregiver support | Dagbanli-ready health and nutrition guidance, clear referral communication |
| Low-connectivity operation | Offline-first architecture — all core clinical flows work without internet |
| Responsible AI | No diagnosis, no prescription, human review of AI output, deterministic safety rules |

## What Judges Should Understand from the Demonstration

1. NorthCare AI solves a real and urgent problem in Northern Ghana's community health system
2. The application works offline from the moment a health worker starts their day
3. Voice-to-Care reduces the burden of manual data entry in the field
4. Danger signs are identified through approved deterministic rules — not unconstrained AI
5. Every AI-extracted field is reviewed and confirmed by the health worker
6. Referrals are tracked end-to-end with an offline-scannable QR passport
7. Nutrition guidance uses locally relevant and affordable foods
8. Dagbanli language support respects the linguistic reality of Northern Ghana
9. The product is designed for real Android devices used by real frontline workers
10. The codebase is clean, documented and ready for continued development

## Competition-Critical Differentiators

- **Not a chatbot demo** — a complete offline-first clinical workflow
- **Not connectivity-dependent** — works in the field without internet
- **Not generic** — designed specifically for Northern Ghana's CHPS system
- **Not irresponsible** — deterministic safety rules, human oversight, no diagnosis
- **Not theoretical** — built for a real Samsung Galaxy S20 Ultra running Android
