# Responsible AI and Clinical Safety

**Purpose:** Permanent safety boundaries for NorthCare AI.  
**Status:** Active  
**Last reviewed:** 2026-08-02  
**Source:** Product principles + competition safety requirements  

## Must not

- Diagnose illnesses  
- Prescribe medicines  
- Calculate medication dosages  
- Replace trained health professionals  
- Fabricate medical protocols or nutrition guidance  
- Fabricate final Dagbanli medical translations  
- Save unreviewed AI extraction as official clinical data  
- Use unconstrained generative AI for emergency decisions  
- Hide uncertainty  

## Safety hierarchy

1. Approved deterministic danger-sign rules  
2. Frontline-worker review  
3. Approved health-content sources  
4. Constrained AI assistance  
5. Clear referral actions  
6. Auditability  
7. Human oversight
